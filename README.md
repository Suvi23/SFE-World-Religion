# ☸ Suffering-Focused Ethics Across World Religions

An interactive single-page website comparing how 10 major world religions
address suffering — analyzed through the lens of Suffering-Focused Ethics (SFE).
Built as an academic project for the SFE course.


## What It Does

- Profiles 10 religions with authentic scriptural references
- Compares them across 7 ethical dimensions (origin of suffering,
  moral obligation, scope of concern, afterlife, liberation, and more)
- Filterable scripture gallery organized by theme
- Animated SFE alignment spectrum chart
- Fully responsive — works on mobile and desktop

## Religions Covered

Buddhism · Hinduism · Jainism · Christianity · Islam
Judaism · Sikhism · Taoism · Zoroastrianism · Confucianism

## Tech Stack

- HTML · CSS · Vanilla JavaScript
- Google Fonts (Cormorant Garamond, Inter)
- No frameworks, no dependencies

### Project Vision

This project is the culmination of extensive research at the intersection of **artificial intelligence, moral philosophy, and comparative religion**. The authors share a conviction that:

> Understanding how humanity's oldest wisdom traditions approached suffering can profoundly inform modern ethical frameworks — and that technology, when guided by compassion, can amplify our capacity to reduce suffering at scale.

The site itself is built on claymorphic, warm design principles that reflect this belief: **ethics should be beautiful, accessible, and compassionate**.



## 📜 Academic References

### Suffering-Focused Ethics (Philosophy)

1. **Popper, Karl** (1945). *The Open Society and Its Enemies*. Routledge.
   - Seminal work introducing "negative utilitarianism"
   
2. **Bentham, Jeremy** (1789). *An Introduction to the Principles of Morals and Legislation*.
   - Foundation: "Can they suffer?" as moral criterion
   
3. **Smart, R. N.** (1958). "Negative Utilitarianism." *Mind*, 67(268).
   - Academic analysis of suffering-focused frameworks
   
4. **Mayerfeld, Jamie** (1999). *Suffering and Moral Responsibility*. Oxford University Press.
   - Comprehensive philosophical treatment
   
5. **Gloor, Lukas** (2016). "The Case for Suffering-Focused Ethics." *Foundational Research Institute*.
   - Contemporary SFE advocacy

### Comparative Religion & Buddhist Studies

6. **Contestabile, Bruno** (2014). "Negative Utilitarianism and Buddhist Intuition." *Contemporary Buddhism*, 15(2).
   - Bridges Buddhist philosophy and SFE
   
7. **Vetter, Tilmann** (1988). *The Ideas and Meditative Practices of Early Buddhism*. Brill.
   - Scholarly analysis of Buddhist suffering-centered ethics
